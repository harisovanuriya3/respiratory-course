# Clinical scene assets

These are teaching visuals, not patient records or quantitative anatomical reconstructions.

- young/adult/senior woman/man: original synthetic clinic portraits from the author's harisovanuriya3/neuro-course-new project, images/patients, generated 2026-10-03. Converted PNG to WebP. No real patient identity.
- thorax-inspiration, thorax-expiration, alveolus-capillary-atlas, spirometry-workstation: original AI-assisted educational artwork from harisovanuriya3/respiratory-course, next-platform/public/visuals, commit 5a7cc8a6ad224902d9353604e0e17879506d54f5. Converted to WebP. Not microscopy or manufacturer photography.
- neonatal-care: original AI-generated educational illustration, 2026-10-21. Prompt: premature newborn resting in a clean incubator, realistic NICU scene, no text/logos/invasive procedure. Not a real infant photograph.
- blood-gas-analyzer: original AI-generated educational equipment illustration, 2026-10-21. Prompt: generic blood-gas analyzer with blank display and closed sample containers, clean laboratory, no labels/brands/procedures. Not a manufacturer's product photograph. Values and graphs are calculated separately by the web application.
- brain-spinal-cord: Z22, Human brain and spinal cord, National Museum of Health and Medicine specimen photograph. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Human_brain_and_spinal_cord.jpg CC BY-SA 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . WebP conversion; displayed with attribution. The converted image remains CC BY-SA 4.0.

Only the thorax endpoint images are crossfaded. Other illustrations remain static while the separately drawn quantitative graph and its cursor animate. Cursor motion is a teaching cycle or parameter scan, not clinical deterioration or recovery. Patient case decisions and records remain in the existing six-stage engine; the adjacent model is explicitly independent of case findings.
