# V35 — integrated reflex physiology

Added a three-language interactive laboratory to modules 17–19 through the existing regulation atlas. Controls: PaCO2, PaO2, lung inflation, proprioceptive movement, and a hypothetical vagal-afferent interruption. Displays independent chemical, vagal inflation-feedback, and proprioceptive contributions and a simplified net teaching index. Includes schematic and three physiology questions in RU/EN/KZ.

Scientific cautions: qualitative illustrative score, not a prediction of ventilation or a clinical decision tool. The Hering–Breuer inflation reflex is more prominent at larger lung volumes and in infants than in healthy adults during quiet breathing. This does not simulate airway irritant and pulmonary C-fiber response patterns, which are complex and may involve cough, apnea, or rapid shallow breathing.
