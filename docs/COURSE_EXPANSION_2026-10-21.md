# Course-wide expansion, 21 October 2026

Scope: respiratory-course only; the neurophysiology repository is unchanged.

## Delivered

- Original additional mechanism and interpretation reading for each of the 25 modules, in RU/EN/KZ (2,705 / 2,762 / 2,292 additional words respectively).
- Twenty extracted lecture images with slide-level provenance. Each module receives a relevant image; some images support more than one topic. Source labels remain in the original image language, alongside localized explanations.
- Image-based guided readings in all 25 media sections. These are interactive explanations, not fabricated recordings or anatomical motion simulations.
- Self-assessment criteria and browser-local marks in all 425 subsections, with a 17-section learning map per chapter. Marks are explicitly distinguished from grades.
- Twenty-five independent clinical examination scenarios, one per module; 461 active unique examination items after deduplication.
- Written case responses for 25 topics, local drafts, submitted-answer locking, model analysis and a four-criterion manual rubric scored 0–100. AI grading is not connected or claimed.
- Reworked module 3 pressure explanation: endpoint comparison, expanded symbols, units, atmospheric reference and distinction between airflow and lung distension. Pretest has written-response fields.

## Verified

- All 1,275 module/section/language combinations rendered: no rendering errors, placeholders, short total sections, missing patient scenario markers, labs without controls or self-assessment pages without controls.
- All 33 test suites passed, including source asset provenance and distinct added exam scenarios.
- Browser: all 75 theory/language pages, images decoded, 390 px mobile width without horizontal overflow; localized image controls, self-mark persistence, written response submission, manual score, reset and exam question selection; no uncaught browser exceptions.

## Remaining limits

Route coverage, text length and automated checks are not independent expert certification of every lesson. Existing laboratories, animations and patient scenarios remain in place; this change does not replace every historical visualization. Embedded lecture labels are not newly translated bitmap artwork. External video playback and voice quality require device-level checks. Written assessment is manual, not AI-based.
