# Comparative audit: respiratory course and neuro-course-new

Audit date: 2026-10-17. Reference snapshot: `neuro-course-new/main` at `59cbd7088401250e2579d2f590bc3dfa2e7b75b8`. The reference repository was inspected from a temporary clone and was not modified.

The respiratory course has complete generation and validation for 25 modules × 17 sections, a larger calculation/exam test suite, 25 mapped laboratories, 27 patient scenarios, and systematic RU/EN/KZ route auditing. Its main gap relative to the neuro course was the absence of a unified per-section learning workspace.

The neuro course has a stronger component model for persistent progress, continuation, bookmarks, notes, drawing, speech controls, structured experiment reflection, and visual patient state. Demo authentication is not appropriate for the respiratory requirement of open access.

Adapted without changing framework or routes: local-only notes and completion state, bookmarks and a last-visited record, a physiology sketch pad with pointer input, privacy guidance, graceful storage failure, and RU/EN/KZ controls.

Remaining staged work: independent scientific and translation review; manual mobile and assistive-technology review; licensed realistic anatomy beyond original diagrams; six-stage branching patients in every module; consistent animation speed/repeat controls; manual external-media licence and link verification.
