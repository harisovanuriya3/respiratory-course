# Version 5: module 4 — mechanics of inspiration and expiration

- Kept 25 modules and 17 sections per module.
- Added module-four.js with RU/EN/KZ physiological explanations, 6 MCQs with feedback, a calculation case, a branching virtual-patient decision and a breathing-cycle lab.
- Lab is an educational one-compartment harmonic approximation: ΔV = VT(1−cos θ)/2, flow = dV/dt, Palv = −R·flow, Ptp = 5 + ΔV/C, Ppl = Palv−Ptp. It is **not** a validated clinical simulator.
- Local animation and graph are original SVG; no third-party medical images embedded. OpenStax and Wikimedia links are provided for further learning, with licensing caveat.
- The rest of the 425 pages are not complete university-level lessons. Professional medical and translation review is pending.
