# V90 — Integration of O₂/CO₂ and acid–base physiology

Added an interactive trilingual integrated laboratory to module 16 (theory, interactive, practice and cases). It computes Henderson–Hasselbalch pH, arterial O₂ content (CaO₂), and oxygen delivery (DO₂), with units, constraints, and a worked anemia case. This is an equilibrium teaching model, not a patient-specific clinical decision system.

Known limitations: sliders independently specify SaO₂ and PaO₂; not all combinations represent physiological Hb–O₂ equilibrium. This is explicitly disclosed. No full 425-lesson scientific peer review is claimed.
